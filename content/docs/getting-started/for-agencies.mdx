Digital agencies with mixed Shopify, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce books can keep **one agent/integration pattern** and swap connectors per client.

## Self-host

OSS packages on npm (`1.0.0-rc.2`). You run Node. Per-client storefront tokens stay in your environment. Start at [Quick start](/docs/getting-started/quick-start).

## Managed pilot

Better Data can provision a hosted runtime for a **paid pilot** (not self-serve). Use this when you want us to operate the gateway across several client shops.

**Not shipped as Cloud Agency GA:** one contract / 100 shops, self-serve provisioning, published SLA, or usage metering you can bill from. Those are productization targets. Do not promise them in a SOW unless a pilot agreement says so.

[Request a managed pilot](mailto:hello@betterdata.co?subject=Agency%20Commerce%20Gateway%20pilot)

## What to tell clients

- Native Shopify UCP / Shop MCP on consumer AI channels **stays**. We do not replace it. [Coexistence](/docs/getting-started/shopify-and-ucp).
- Connector behavior is **not** identical. Share the [capability matrix](/docs/getting-started/connector-capabilities).
- Shopify Storefront cannot look up orders (Admin API required).
