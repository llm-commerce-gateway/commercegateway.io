Commerce Gateway is **open infrastructure**. It is not a shopping concierge SKU and not Shopify UCP.

- **Merchant-owned experiences** — brands and retailers. Self-hosted OSS. ([below](#1-merchant-owned-commerce-experiences))
- **Build once across platforms** — developers and agencies. Self-hosted OSS. ([below](#2-build-once-across-commerce-platforms))
- **Federated multi-merchant** — multi-brand and collectives. OSS registry protocol. ([below](#3-multiple-merchants-and-storefronts))
- **Embed in your software** — ISVs and vertical SaaS. OSS or hosted pilot. ([For software vendors](/docs/getting-started/for-software-vendors))
- **Agency client portfolios** — digital agencies. OSS or hosted pilot. ([For agencies](/docs/getting-started/for-agencies))

**Horizontal:** keep commerce connectivity stable while you change **Anthropic, OpenAI, Grok, or Gemini**. Not every model behaves the same.

**Not a Gateway OSS use case:** Boss Loops “governed checkout.” High-value approvals (POs, discounts, replenishment) are a Better Data platform expansion.

Native Shopify UCP / Shop MCP can **coexist**. See [Shopify, UCP, and this gateway](/docs/getting-started/shopify-and-ucp).

## 1. Merchant-owned commerce experiences

Power product search, details, availability, and **supported** cart or checkout flows on a site, mobile app, or custom agent you host.

- Control the application, merchandising logic, and customer relationship — not merely “avoid Meta.”
- Self-hosting does **not** by itself give full customer attribution (identity, session, order-source tracking).
- Shopify `create_order` is **checkout handoff**, not a completed Storefront order. See the [capability matrix](/docs/getting-started/connector-capabilities).

## 2. Build once across commerce platforms

One normalized MCP interface across **supported** Shopify Storefront, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce operations.

Do not assume identical auth, variants, carts, or orders. The value is reusable agent logic **where the backend supports the tool**, plus one connector maintenance cycle.

This story holds even if every platform ships native UCP tomorrow — those channels serve consumer AI surfaces; this gateway serves **your** application.

## 3. Multiple merchants and storefronts

[Registry MCP](/docs/registry/setup) discovers merchants. [Gateway MCP](/docs/getting-started/quick-start) executes **per merchant**.

Not included: a single cart across merchants, consolidated payments, split settlements, or centralized inventory reservation.
